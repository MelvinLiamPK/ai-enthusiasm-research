## ② §5 — regression specs

(`specs/specs_report.md`, `specs/coefficients_all_specs.csv`; strong-match default,
SEs clustered by firm, locked zero-imputation rule.)

- **§5.1 sub-annual returns ~ general sentiment.** `mom_net_sentiment_strong` → quarterly &
  monthly calendar returns (contemporaneous and one-period-ahead), time and firm+time FE.
  **β ≈ 0 and insignificant everywhere** (βs ~1e-5). No detectable monthly/quarterly return
  predictability from general sentiment, and the contemporaneous "doing-well→positive-posts"
  link doesn't show at this frequency.
- **§5.2 five-year long-difference.** Δ5 `ln_tobins_q` ~ Δ5 `ai_sent_new`, **winsorized on
  the differenced variables** (year FE, clustered). **β = 0.00092\*\*\*** — *larger* than the
  year-to-year within-firm FE estimate (~0.0007), consistent with high-frequency
  attenuation. Δ5 `ai_post_share_strong` enters positive but insignificant.
- **§5.3 size-weighted headline.** `ln_tobins_q ~ ai_sent_new`, weight = **lagged total
  assets**. Unweighted reproduces John (0.0035\*\*\* / 0.00067\*\*\*). Asset-weighted: the
  time-FE effect holds (0.0033\*\*\*) but the **firm+time-FE effect collapses to ~1e-4 (ns)**
  → the within-firm AI-sentiment→Q effect is concentrated in **smaller** firms.
- **§5.4 AI-keyword time series** (`specs/ai_keyword_timeseries.png`): AI-post share rose
  **×5.0** post-ChatGPT (strong: 1.30%→6.54%), corroborating the ~5.7× face-validity result.
- **§5.5 LOESS** (`specs/loess_*.png`): Q rises with AI sentiment; AI-post share rises
  monotonically with firm size — the linear specs aren't masking nonlinearity.

### Length / intensity horse-race (John's post-length signal) — **VERDICT: not incremental**
Three intensity measures (`ai_net_pos_words`, `ai_mean_post_len`, within-person `ai_rel_len`;
AI posts only, strong, `lm_word_count>0`) + four specs (a–d). After conditioning on the
extensive margin (`ai_post_share_strong`) + density (`ai_sent_new`), length/intensity adds
**no signal under firm FE**: density×length interaction p=0.68; `ai_net_pos_words` p=0.41,
ΔAIC +0.6, ΔR² −0.0008. Cross-sectionally (time-FE only) longer AI posts associate with
*lower* Q, fully absorbed by firm FE. → **Tested, not incremental.** Keep density as the
headline; re-test in v2 on the expanded corpus (small AI-posting N=5,547 is the main caveat).
